Is HubSpot the only platform you work with?✦

HubSpot is our core expertise. We also connect the tools your business relies on, including forms, calendars, analytics, advertising platforms and specialist apps.

Can you improve an existing HubSpot portal?✦

Yes. A project can begin with an audit, cleanup and prioritized optimization roadmap before any larger rebuild is considered.

Do you work with international teams?✦

Yes. LJ Studio works remotely and supports English- and German-speaking businesses with clear documentation and structured handovers.

What happens after implementation?✦

You can choose a documented handover, team training or an ongoing optimization partnership for reporting, workflows and strategic development.
